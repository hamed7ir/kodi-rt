script.module.pil for kodi-rt: Pillow 12.0.0 (LICENSE in this folder: the MIT-CMU / HPND licence), built from Kodi's
pinned source with clang-cl for Windows. Its C modules (lib/PIL/*.pyd) also contain these libraries, statically
linked, each under its own licence:

  libjpeg-turbo 3.1.4.1   in _imaging.pyd     IJG licence and the Modified (3-clause) BSD licence
  zlib 1.3.2              in _imaging.pyd     zlib licence
  FreeType 2.14.2         in _imagingft.pyd   FreeType Licence (FTL), or GPL-2.0 at your choice
  HarfBuzz 14.2.0         in _imagingft.pyd   "Old MIT" licence
  FriBidi 1.0.17          in _imagingft.pyd   GNU LGPL 2.1 or later
  raqm (Pillow's copy)    in _imagingft.pyd   MIT licence

The licence texts are in each library's source archive, all of them Kodi's pinned versions (Kodi's
tools/depends/target/<library>/*-VERSION files, the archives from Kodi's mirror): libjpeg-turbo LICENSE.md and
README.ijg, zlib's README / zlib.h, FreeType LICENSE.TXT and docs/FTL.TXT, HarfBuzz COPYING, FriBidi COPYING, and
Pillow's src/thirdparty/raqm/COPYING. kodi-rt's scripts that build these modules (cross/cmake/pillow,
scripts/pil-build.sh) and the source versions are part of the kodi-rt project.
